    Campaign detail

    How to read this demo
    Campaign
    A group of scanning sources that the engine links by shared machinery: target ports, header fingerprint, probing strategy and operational behavior. Timing is not used for grouping; it is reserved for the coordination test.
    Orchestrated
    The campaign's bots are active together more than both nulls allow: each bot's own activity shifted by whole days, and activity-matched peers on the same port. Independent campaigns share machinery but show no coordination beyond those nulls.
    Undetermined
    The campaign makes up most of its port's sources, so there are too few peers to run the population null; coordination is not judged.
    Synchrony z
    How far the campaign's temporal synchrony sits above the null model, in standard deviations.
    First report
    The hourly batch after which the online engine first reported the campaign. During replay a campaign appears only from that hour on.
    Probing strategy
    Each dot is a sampled destination: its time, and its relative position inside the monitored block. Addresses are never shown.
    Rate and coverage
    Packets per hour per bot as seen by the telescope (median), and the share of the monitored block the campaign probed.
    openjev
    An AI decision model that scores each option from its logits and calibrates the result. Its outputs are probabilities, not ground truth.
